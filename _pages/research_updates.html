--- layout: landing permalink: / title: "Narae Lee's Research Website" author_profile: false lede: I am an economist conducting research at the intersection of Infrastructure, Labor, Development, and Health Economics. My papers investigate how the infrastructure development cycle shapes jobs in developing economies and for socially underrepresented populations, with a recent focus on the clean and digital transition of infrastructure.

Methodologically, I combine applied microeconomic causal inference with computational social science tools, such as geospatial and social network analyses, to closely track policy impacts. My research leverages diverse data streams to capture policy and recipient dynamics in real time, including administrative records, digital device data, and field high-frequency surveys. Trained in public policy analysis, I have been working with diverse policy, operational, and funding partners to produce timely and policy-relevant economic evidence. ---
