--- layout: landing permalink: / title: "Research Updates" author_profile: false lede: I am an economist working at the intersection of Infrastructure, Labor, Development, and Health Economics. My research investigates how the infrastructure development cycle shapes jobs, with a recent focus on the clean energy and digital transition in developing economies.
Methodologically, I combine economic causal inference with computational social science to conduct high-frequency tracking of policy impacts. To capture these dynamics in real time, my empirical work leverages diverse data streams, including administrative records, digital device data, and field surveys. Trained in public policy engagements, I have been working with diverse operational and funding partners to produce policy-relevant economic evidence. ---
